**Conventions**
- `[S#]` refers to the source list at the end. Each source has a URL and a publication date.
- **(U)** marks something unverified: a single non-authoritative source, a figure that conflicts with another source, or general industry knowledge without a fetched source. Treat these items as evidence level E3/E4.
- Sales figures are calendar-year 2025 unless stated otherwise. Where sources disagree, both figures are shown.

**Why Chinese premium brands take share from German brands in China (evidence):**

1. **Intelligent driving.** Chinese premium NEV buyers cite automated driving as the #1 purchase reason, ahead of smart cabin and styling [S54]. ADAS has up to 2× the purchase influence of Western markets, and 50% of Chinese shoppers would switch brand for better ADAS [S43]. "Insufficient intelligent driving experience" is cited for the BBA decline [S49].
2. **Smart cockpit and ecosystem.** Buyers expect phone-like UX, voice, and ecosystem integration (Huawei HarmonyOS, Xiaomi HyperOS). 45% of luxury owners say NEVs above RMB 300k need full smart connectivity [S54]. Even BMW's China Neue Klasse uses Momenta ADAS, Huawei HiCar and an Alibaba LLM [S5][S55].
3. **Brand heritage matters less.** Heritage only slightly outranks safety and handling as a premium definer, and matters less among high-income tier-1 buyers [S54][S56].
4. **Rear-seat luxury for the money.** The M9 and S800 offer zero-gravity seats, fridge, projector and 3.3 m+ wheelbases at prices below German flagships [S29][S6].
5. **Electrification gap.** BEV is only about 5% of BMW China sales against a 46% EV market [S54]. EREV/PHEV options give range without anxiety [S48].
6. **Innovation over discounts.** McKinsey: steep discounts gave marginal growth, and innovation wins [S56].

### Brand-fit guardrails (for `fit_checks.brand`)

**All segments**
- **Fits BMW (pass):** precise steering and handling, rear-wheel-drive-biased feel and xDrive, responsive powertrains; driver-centric HMI (head-up display, Panoramic Vision, physical shortcut controls, iDrive-style haptic control); efficiency and range as "dynamics"; sporty-elegant design; M and M Performance variants; high-quality OTA and natural voice that *works*; ADAS that reduces burden while keeping the driver in charge.
- **Warn:** features that are "comfort-only" with no link to driving pleasure (acceptable but not differentiating); market-specific ecosystem partners (Huawei/Alibaba) acceptable in China only; grille-size escalation (polarising; region-specific).
- **Fail (contradicts the brand):** paywalling hardware already in the car (heated-seat precedent); touch-only access to core driving functions; soft, numb chassis tuning as default; gimmick recognition tech with no customer problem; copying the Chinese "living room on wheels" so far that the driver role is diminished in the 1 and 5 Series.

| Segment | Fits BMW (pass) | Warn | Contradicts (fail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Series** | Hot-hatch driving fun (M135), compact premium quality, intuitive physical controls, efficient BEV/PHEV option, youthful digital UX and app ecosystem | Pure value or feature stuffing to fight Chinese entrants; FWD-only perception | Cheap interior trim; touch-only climate (already criticised); losing dynamic edge vs Golf GTI |
| **5 Series / i5** | "Business athlete": dynamics plus long-distance comfort, top ADAS on the highway, fast charging and range, rear comfort in the China LWB, quiet cabin | China-only rear "boss" features in the G68 (pass for CN, warn for EU/US); third-party cockpit OS (CN only) | Sacrificing handling for float-ride everywhere; global commitment to an EREV range-extender identity **(PM judgement)** |
| **7 Series / i7** | Driver-*and*-chauffeur duality, Theatre Screen and rear lounge, Executive Lounge seats, craftsmanship, quiet BEV, L2+/L3 assisted driving | Matching Maextro/M9 gadgets one-for-one (fridge, projector) unless executed at BMW quality | Overt bling with no taste (grille-size controversy); dropping driving dynamics entirely (the S-Class owns pure comfort) |
