# BMW Group Report 2025 — extract for the knowledge base

**Source:** BMW Group Report 2025 (published Mar 2026, PDF created 2 Mar 2026, 434 pages), https://www.bmwgroup.com/content/dam/grpw/websites/bmwgroup_com/ir/downloads/en/2026/bericht/BMW-Group-Report-2025-en.pdf. Retrieved 2026-10-10.
**Trust tier:** T1 (official OEM annual report, audited parts by PwC). **Page numbers** = printed page = PDF page.
**Why:** BMW support (2026-10-10) recommended the report for (1) market & sales data and (2) product strategy & R&D. Only these two areas are extracted; the financial statements are skipped.

**How to use:**
- **Evidence:** these are evidence for *market context and BMW's strategic direction*.
- **Not feasibility:** they are **not** engineering feasibility proof, and they don't turn engineering topics into customer requirements (scope: `challenge-brief.md` → Scope tensions, Q-010).
- **Rounding:** figures are as printed; the report rounds per commercial practice (p. 7).
- **Supersedes:** this final report supersedes the provisional January 2026 press-release figures (`portfolio-reference.md` §3).

### 1.4 Electrification / EV adoption

| Metric | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group BEV share of deliveries | 4.1% | 9.0% | 14.7% | 17.4% | **17.9%** | 9 |
| Group electrified (BEV+PHEV) share | 13.0% | 18.1% | 22.2% | 24.2% | **26.1%** | 12 |
| Group BEV units | 103,854 | 215,752 | 375,716 | 426,536 | 442,059 (+3.6%) | 12 |

- **Group PHEV:** 200,012 (+20.0%), share 8.1%. Electrified total 642,071 (+8.2%) (p. 77).
- **BMW brand:** BEV 335,528 (**−8.9%**, 15.5% of the brand); PHEV 199,883 (**+21.8%**, 9.2%) (p. 79). BEV growth came from MINI (105,529 BEV, +87.9%, 36.6% of MINI; p. 80). **PHEV is BMW's growing electrified powertrain.**
- **Model-level adoption:**
  - iX1 was the BMW brand's most successful BEV; >25% of X1 deliveries were BEV.
  - Almost half of 4 Series (Gran Coupé) deliveries were the i4.
  - iX3 (NK) preorders "extend well into 2026" (p. 78).
- **Production:** electrified 597,309 (−8.2%), of which BEV 391,693 (−18.7%) (p. 61).
- **Targets and outlook:**
  - Strategic target: **>50% BEV share of deliveries by 2030**, "albeit with regional differences" (p. 3, 46).
  - 2026 outlook: BEV share "in line with last year's level" (p. 263).
  - Markets are "highly fragmented … demand, especially for electric vehicles, also depends on regional factors": charging infrastructure, cost benefits, energy prices (p. 44).

### 1.5 2026 outlook (as of Mar 2026, p. 263–264)
- **Deliveries:** Group deliveries on a par with 2025. Growth potential in Europe and the USA.
- **China:**
  - BMW "can reach the prior year's level" by stabilising transaction prices.
  - But the market is expected *below* 2025: EV subsidy cuts (especially ≤ CNY 150k) and price regulation (SAMR, Feb 2026).
  - Reality check: actual H1 2026 China was −20.4% (`portfolio-reference.md` §3.5), so the outlook was optimistic.
- **USA:** BEV sales expected to go **down** due to regulatory changes.
- **Europe:** expected to provide most of the market growth (CO₂ rules, subsidies for electrified vehicles).
- **Spending:** R&D expenditure, capex and costs "will be reduced further" in 2026 (p. 264).

### 1.6 Target persona & market prioritization — what the report does and doesn't give
- **It does:**
  - Market weights (§1.2 share of sales).
  - Powertrain trend per brand (§1.4).
  - Model-series momentum (§1.3).
  - BMW's view of customer trends (p. 42):
    - customers shape vehicle requirements around "the digital ecosystems they use daily";
    - "software updates and upgrades have become standard";
    - automated driving is "one of the key expectation[s]", with regional differences in speed;
    - vehicle ownership depends on income, household size and location.
- **It doesn't:** **no demographic/persona data, no per-model regional split, no customer-satisfaction scores** (p. 188–189 describe the survey *processes* only).

## 2. Product strategy & R&D

### 2.1 R&D investment priorities

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| R&D expenditure | €9,078 m | **€8,319 m** (−8.4%) | 70, 71, 81 |
| R&D expenditure ratio (% of revenues) | 6.4% | 6.2% | 10, 71 |
| Capitalisation rate | 38.8% | 41.2% | 71 |

- **Focus:** R&D is "related primarily to the **cross-series digitalisation and electrification** of the vehicle fleet". It includes development of **NEUE KLASSE models such as the BMW iX3 and the successors to the BMW X5 and the BMW 7 Series** (p. 70, 81).
- **2026:** R&D expenditure "will be reduced further" (p. 264).
- **Strategic focus areas:**
  1. Technology-open drivetrains with a strong electromobility focus.
  2. Digitalisation of customer interfaces and related products/services.
  3. Sustainability / circular economy (p. 44).
- **Quality programme** "Q3 – Passion for Perfection": quality and reliability are named key factors for customer satisfaction (p. 45).
- **Partners:**
  - Momenta (China ADAS).
  - Qualcomm (automated driving, launching 2026 with the iX3, "subsequently … rolled out across the entire BMW model range"; ~1,300 specialists).
  - Valeo (automated parking) (p. 47, 54, 58).

### 2.2 NEUE KLASSE platform strategy
- **Roll-out:**
  - The NK "technology toolkit and the new design language will be rolled out across the **entire vehicle portfolio, encompassing all drivetrain types, by the end of the decade**" (p. 46).
  - "By 2027, **40 new and revised models** featuring NEUE KLASSE technologies and the new design language" (p. 53).
- **Architecture:**
  - The first NK model (iX3, built in Debrecen) is based on the **"new cluster architecture (NCAR)", focused entirely on BEVs** (p. 46). This is BMW's own platform name; trade press uses others (`portfolio-reference.md` §1).
  - The aim is to "gradually transfer the new vehicle architecture to the global production network over the next years" (p. 59).
- **Model timing:** second NK model i3: Munich, 2026 (p. 53, 59). Munich goes 100% electric by 2027 (p. 47). Spartanburg: ≥6 all-electric X models by 2030 (p. 59).
- **Gen6 eDrive vs. the fifth generation:**
  - Range +30% (iX3 up to **805 km WLTP**).
  - Charging speed +30%.
  - Cylindrical cells with +20% volumetric energy density (p. 54).
  - Gen6 battery assembly: Debrecen (2025), then Irlbach-Straßkirchen, Woodruff/Spartanburg, San Luis Potosí, Shenyang (p. 47, 59).
- **Customer-facing NK technologies:**
  - **BMW Panoramic iDrive:** Panoramic Vision across the full windscreen width, optional 3D head-up display, 17.9″ central display (p. 53).
  - **BMW Operating System X:** in-house, AOSP-based; "high degree of personalisation, regular over-the-air updates and seamless integration of the My BMW app" (p. 53).
  - **BMW Intelligent Personal Assistant with LLM technology:** proactive, personalised routines (p. 53). Alexa Custom Assistant-based since 2024, retrofittable via Remote Software Upgrade (p. 58); Alexa+ (p. 29).
  - **Heart of Joy:** driving-dynamics control unit; "enhances the driving experience" plus efficiency/range (p. 46).
  - **Zonal E/E architecture, "software-defined vehicle"** (p. 54).
  - **Highway & City Assistant:** up to 130 km/h on selected highways; stops at red lights and pulls away in Germany. First UN R171 (DCAS) approval in Germany. **Symbiotic Drive:** steering + braking input without switching the assistant off (p. 54).
  - **Bidirectional charging** on the iX3: V2H, V2G (E.ON offer from Feb 2026), V2L (p. 56).
- **NK in China** ("in China, for China and with China"; p. 28–29, 53–54):
  - iX3 Long Wheelbase: premiere Auto China, April 2026; market entry H2 2026.
  - OS X China has ~70% locally developed software.
  - Amap navigation.
  - LLMs from Alibaba and DeepSeek.
  - Huawei Digital Key, HiCar, and the **My BMW App based on HarmonyOS NEXT**.
  - Momenta L2+ "all scenarios" ADAS tailored to Chinese roads and "user expectations".
  - Media feedback on the iX3 China version: "digital features proving particularly popular" (p. 28).
- **Other drivetrains:**
  - The X5 (presented 2026) will offer five drivetrains: BEV, PHEV, petrol, diesel, hydrogen from 2028. iX5 Hydrogen in 2028 (p. 28, 46, 56).
  - Combustion engines continue alongside BEVs (p. 42, 46).
  - 16 BEV models on offer by end of 2025. iX up to 701 km WLTP; i4 facelift up to 613 km WLTP (p. 46).

### 2.3 ConnectedDrive & My BMW App
- **What the report says (qualitative only):**
  - **Remote Software Upgrade** delivers new features in "infotainment, driver assistance, comfort and safety". Availability depends on "country, model, equipment and software version" (p. 53).
  - **ConnectedDrive upgrades** let customers "buy additional digital services after the initial vehicle purchase" (p. 53). For brand-fit rules on paywalled hardware, see `competitor-landscape.md` §4.
  - 5G and cloud-based services (p. 53).
  - The My BMW app shows driving-style efficiency and is a charging/payment and support channel (p. 44, 55, 189).
- **Charging network access** via the app/navigation (p. 55):
  - 3.4 M charging points worldwide; >1 M in Europe.
  - IONITY: 840 stations / >5,900 points.
  - IONNA (US): 80 stations / >760 points; NACS adapter in 2025.
  - IONCHI (China): >460 stations / >2,500 points.
- **No usage metrics:** **the report contains no ConnectedDrive or My BMW App usage metrics** (users, downloads, upgrade take-rates, OTA reach). Don't cite any such numbers to this source. If needed, research them separately (T1/T2, with a citation).
